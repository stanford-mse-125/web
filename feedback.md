## Quick feedback on course notes

Spotted a typo? Found an explanation confusing? Have a suggestion for a better example?

Use this short form to send us feedback on any chapter or section of the course notes.
You can also find a link to this form via the QR code shown in lecture slides
and at the end of each chapter.
